# Lua Inspector Fields

Open this folder as a project in SatEngine. Both cubes use `Scripts/spin.lua`.
**Default Cube** starts at 45 degrees per second. **Fast Cube** has a saved
override of 120 degrees per second.

1. Select **Fast Cube** and expand **Lua Script: spin.lua** in the Inspector.
2. Press **Play**. Change `speed` or toggle `spinning` and watch the right cube
   respond while the left cube keeps spinning.
3. Click **Reset** beside `speed` to return to 45 degrees per second.
4. Stop Play and choose **Save Changes** to keep your edits or **Discard Changes**
   to restore the starting values.
